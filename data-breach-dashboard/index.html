What this means for Black Shard's clients

Health is the top target

— lodge the most notifications. Medical practices such as cardiology clinics hold sensitive data and need Essential Eight controls and staff training.

Stolen logins are the main way in

Phishing and stolen credentials make up the largest cyber incident types. Multi-factor authentication, one of the Essential Eight, directly reduces this risk.

People cause a large share

Human error caused — of notifications in Jul–Dec 2025, such as emails sent to the wrong person. Training and data-handling policies matter as much as technology.

Breaches are bigger

Breaches affecting over 1 million people nearly tripled. Fast incident response and breach remediation limit the damage when an attack succeeds.
