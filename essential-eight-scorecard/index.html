Prepared by Kishan Akabari · Based on the Australian Signals Directorate (ASD) Essential Eight maturity model (November 2023) and its ISM mapping. This is a self-assessment aid, not a formal Essential Eight assessment.
How the scoring works and sources
  • Each statement summarises one or more ASD requirements; the ISM control numbers are shown under each question.
  • A strategy reaches a level only when every requirement at that level and the levels below is fully met. The overall level is the lowest level across all eight strategies, which is how ASD reports overall maturity.
  • For patching operating systems, Maturity Level Two has no requirements beyond Level One, so meeting Level One also meets Level Two.
  • Logging, log analysis and incident response requirements that ASD repeats across strategies at Levels Two and Three are folded into the most relevant questions.
